Aloe Vera Plant Information

Scientific Name: Aloe barbadensis miller

Common Names: Aloe Vera, True Aloe

Family: Asphodelaceae (Liliaceae)

Native To: Arabian Peninsula

Most Found In: Tropical climates worldwide, including Africa, India, and the Caribbean

Water Requirements

Low watering is required.

Water every 3 weeks and even less in winter.

Overwatering can cause root rot, while underwatering can cause the leaves to shrivel.

Uses of Aloe Vera

1. Medicinal Uses:

Used to treat skin conditions like burns, wounds, and irritations.

Known for its soothing and moisturizing properties.

2. Cosmetic Uses:

Commonly used in skincare and haircare products.

Helps in hydrating and nourishing the skin.

3. Health Benefits:

Used in dietary supplements and health drinks.

Believed to aid digestion and boost immunity.

Growth Conditions

Soil: Well-draining, sandy soil.

Sunlight: Prefers bright, indirect sunlight.

Temperature: Thrives in warm climates (20–30°C).
